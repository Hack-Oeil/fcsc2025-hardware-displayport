Challenge #580

DisplayPort

With my ultra-fast differential probe, I managed to measure a differential signal between pins #1 and #3 of the DisplayPort connector plugged into my PC. It is a binary serial signal at 1.62 Gigabits per second. I pre-processed the signal and was able to faithfully reconstruct the bit sequence transmitted by the video card (which is compatible with the DisplayPort 1.2 standard). I saved these bits into a binary file, in the following format: 8 bits per byte, the most significant bit being the first bit transmitted (MSB first).

I know that when I captured the signal, my (only) screen was configured in 800x600 75Hz 24 bits/pixel RGB 1-lane, and was displaying a picture of my office colleagues holding the flag in their (small) hands.

I lost this wonderful photo, will you be able to reconstruct it from this capture?
